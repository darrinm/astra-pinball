How to play

Hold Space to pull the plunger, then release. Use ← / → or A / D to flip. On touch screens, use the bottom controls.

Ramps: shoot either glowing entrance. Alternate mint and strawberry ramps to raise your multiplier up to 5×.

Sugar Rush: hit all five colored drop targets for 30 seconds of double scoring.

Multiball: shoot the right lock three times. With multiple balls active, the castle scoop awards a growing jackpot.

Nudge: press N to save a dangerous bounce. Three quick nudges tilt the table and disable flippers until the ball drains. Each fresh ball has 12 seconds of ball save.

Press P to pause. Your best score saves on this device.
